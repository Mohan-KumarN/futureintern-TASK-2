### Power BI Step-by-Step Instructions (Task 2)
To create a professional Retention Dashboard in Power BI, follow these steps:

Step 1: Data Setup

1. Open Power BI Desktop and import customer_churn_data.csv .
2. In Power Query (Transform Data):
   - Set IsChurned to a Whole Number (0 or 1).
   - Ensure SignupDate and ChurnDate are Date types.
3. DAX Measure (Optional but Recommended) : Create a measure for Churn Rate: Churn Rate % = DIVIDE(SUM(Table[IsChurned]), COUNT(Table[CustomerID]))
Step 2: Executive KPI Cards

1. Add Card visuals for:
   - Churn Rate % (using the DAX measure above).
   - Average Tenure (Average of TenureMonths ).
   - Total Revenue (Sum of TotalCharges ).
Step 3: Churn Drivers (Visuals)

1. Bar Chart (Horizontal) : Use ChurnReason on the Y-axis and CustomerID (Count) on the X-axis. Filter the visual to only show IsChurned = 1 .
2. Clustered Column Chart : Use Plan on the X-axis and Churn Rate % on the Y-axis to see which subscription tiers are failing.
3. Scatter Chart : Use SupportTickets on the X-axis and TenureMonths on the Y-axis, with IsChurned as the Legend . This shows the "danger zone" where high tickets lead to early exits.
Step 4: Cohort & Lifecycle

1. Line Chart : Use SignupDate (Month/Year) on the X-axis and CustomerID (Count) on the Y-axis to see acquisition trends.
2. Treemap : Use UsageFrequency as the Group and CustomerID (Count) as the Values to see the health of your active user base.
Step 5: Professional Formatting

1. Theme : Use the "Storm" or "High Contrast" theme for a data-heavy professional look.
2. Slicers : Add slicers for Plan , UsageFrequency , and SignupDate .
3. Conditional Formatting : In your Bar Charts, set the color to change to Red if the Churn Rate exceeds a certain threshold (e.g., 30%).
You can now use these instructions to build the dashboard and present the Retention_Analysis_Report.md insights interactively!
