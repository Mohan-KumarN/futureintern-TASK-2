### Project Overview
This task focused on identifying churn patterns and retention drivers for a subscription-based business. I have generated a dataset of 2,000 customers and analyzed their behavior to provide actionable business recommendations.

### Implemented Solution
1. Data Generation

- Created customer_churn_data.csv using generate_churn_data.py .
- Dataset includes: Tenure, Churn Reasons, Support Tickets, Usage Frequency, and Subscription Plans (Basic, Standard, Premium).
2. Churn Analysis Engine

- Developed analyze_churn.py to extract key insights:
  - Churn Rate : 29.05%
  - Average CLV : $212.95
  - Top Churn Reason : Price too high
  - Correlation : High support ticket volume correlates with increased churn risk.
3. Visualizations & Reporting

- Generated professional charts in the assets folder.
- Compiled a comprehensive Retention_Analysis_Report.md with deep-dive insights and strategic recommendations.
